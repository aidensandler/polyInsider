Alerts and copy-trading tests

Could you profit by following flagged wallets?

We replayed July 2025 to November 2026 and asked: if an alert had fired when a skilled-looking wallet traded, and you had copied that trade after a delay, would you have made money on later markets? Every number below uses only information available at the time of the trade.

Bottom line. The machine-learning alert model did not work out of sample: it lost about 20% per dollar staked. A much simpler rule (follow wallets whose past evidence score is at least 3, with 10 or more past events) earned a modest positive return of about 8% to 14%, but the uncertainty is wide, a few wallets drive much of it, and in the final untouched window random trades also made money. This is a research result, not a trading signal.

One-shot test on a fresh window

Oct to Nov 2026, never used for any decision

The rule was frozen before this window. At a 5-minute delay it returned +8.4% (interval -0.8% to +18.0%), while random trades in the same window returned +4.1%. The edge over random is therefore small and not statistically clear.

How to read this

Alert. A trade by a wallet that the scoring system considers skilled, using only that wallet's earlier resolved events. Copy return. Buy the same side at the market price a set delay later, hold to resolution, and divide profit by dollars staked. Delay. 0 minutes is an upper bound you could not actually achieve; 5 minutes and 1 hour are realistic.

Why the model failed but the simple rule held up. The model learned from a short, small set of flagged wallets and did not carry over to later periods. A single evidence score with a long-enough track record is less prone to that kind of overfitting.

Cautions

  • Copying a trade can move the price against you; the results include a 1 cent test but not full market impact.
  • The rule is concentrated: one wallet supplies a large share of alerts and profit. See the "without top wallet" figure in the repository results.
  • All intervals are wide. Agreement between strategies (for example following several wallets at once) was not established.
  • Flagged wallets are not proven insiders; they are wallets whose past results were unusually good.

Full code, tests and result files are in the analysis/ folder of the repository.
