How it works

  1. Score. Each wallet gets an evidence score from its earlier resolved events only, comparing its entry prices with how events actually ended.
  2. Flag. Wallets whose score is too high to be explained by luck, after correcting for testing thousands of wallets, are flagged.
  3. Test forward. Flags are frozen, then checked on later markets, including a control group of crypto up/down markets where informed trading is unlikely.
Read the full method →

What we found

Flagged wallets keep outperforming on later events, and they trade differently: more of their money lands right before big price moves.

Following their trades earned about 8% per dollar staked in our forward tests, with wide uncertainty and a few wallets doing much of the work. Results are research findings, not trading advice.
