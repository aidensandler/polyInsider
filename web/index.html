The research question

Earlier outcomes → later outcomes

Can we identify wallets with unusually strong returns using only information available before a cut-off date, and do those wallets perform better than comparable wallets on later events?

Our previous historical analysis suggests persistent differences in performance, not proof of insider trading. We separate wallet scoring from the new event-level probability forecast.

What the TA asked for

Our new contribution

Before resolution: observe public trades and market prices; score participating wallets using only their earlier resolved events; output a forecast and save its timestamp.

After resolution: match the saved prediction to a verified outcome and compare forecast accuracy with the original market probability.

Unlike a retrospective leaderboard, the tracker creates an audit trail that cannot be silently replaced by hindsight.
