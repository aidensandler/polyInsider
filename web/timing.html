Trader timing

When do the flagged wallets trade?

We measured when flagged wallets place their trades compared with other experienced traders: how long before a market resolves, how far through its life, and relative to the market's biggest price moves. Then we checked whether the price moved in their favour afterwards. Everything below covers markets after the 1 June 2026 cut-off, using wallets that were flagged only from earlier data.

Bottom line. Flagged wallets do trade a little differently: a bit more of their money lands in the final day before resolution, and noticeably more lands just before or during the market's biggest price jumps, and the price tends to keep moving their way for hours afterwards. But most of their money (roughly 75% to 85%) goes in more than a day before the event, the differences are modest, and we cannot rule out that their own buying moved the price. This is a pattern, not proof that anyone traded on private information.
