05 / Trader Timing Analysis

When do flagged traders trade?

We examine whether historically flagged Polymarket wallets concentrate their trading activity at particular times, and compare their behavior with other participants in the same markets.

Research question. Do wallets identified by historical trading performance exhibit distinctive trading patterns by hour of day, day of week, or proximity to recorded information times?

Why timing matters

Research interpretation

If historically successful wallets trade more heavily at particular times, this could reveal differences in their trading behavior.

Timing patterns may also reflect ordinary market activity, liquidity, and event schedules.

How we measure activity

Historical transactions

We use timestamped archived trade records and Aiden's V4 wallet classifications.

The two groups are compared within the same markets. The displayed frequencies are normalized within each group.

Important limitation. Historically flagged wallets are not proven insiders. Several information times are approximate scheduled event times rather than independently verified first-public-announcement times. These descriptive charts do not establish causation or private information.
